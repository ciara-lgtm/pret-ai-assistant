# Equipment Safety Escalation — Mock Internal Knowledge

> **MOCK / FICTIONAL DOCUMENT — Technical Interview POC only.**
> This document does not represent actual Pret policies or procedures.

## Purpose

This document provides fictional guidance for immediate safety concerns involving store equipment.

## Immediate Safety Concerns

Treat the following as a safety concern:

- Smoke coming from equipment
- Burning smell
- Sparks
- Exposed or damaged wiring
- Damaged electrical plugs or cables
- Signs of electrical arcing
- Significant or uncontrolled water leakage
- Equipment becoming unusually hot
- Any condition that could create an electrical, fire, or injury risk

## Stop Use

If an immediate safety concern is identified:

1. **Stop using the equipment immediately.**
2. Keep employees and customers away from the affected equipment where appropriate.
3. Do not attempt to investigate the internal cause.
4. Notify the **Shift Manager or Duty Manager immediately**.
5. Follow the store's established emergency or safety process where applicable.

Do not restart the equipment simply to check whether the problem has disappeared.

## Repairs

Employees must **not perform unauthorised repairs**.

Do not open electrical panels, repair wiring, replace internal components, or bypass safety mechanisms.

The manager is responsible for escalating the incident to the appropriate maintenance or safety support.

**Safety concerns always take priority over routine coffee machine troubleshooting.**
