# Equipment Replacement Process — Mock Internal Knowledge

> **MOCK / FICTIONAL DOCUMENT — Technical Interview POC only.**
> This document does not represent actual Pret policies or procedures.

## Purpose

This document describes a fictional process for deciding whether faulty store equipment should be repaired or replaced.

## Raising a Request

The **Shift Manager or Duty Manager** is responsible for raising a maintenance or equipment replacement request.

Employees should report faulty equipment to the manager rather than independently creating replacement orders.

The request should contain:

- Store or location
- Equipment type
- Equipment identifier, if available
- Description of the fault
- Relevant error codes
- Date the fault was identified
- Whether the equipment is currently usable
- Any previous maintenance or repair reference, if known

## Repair vs Replacement

Maintenance support should initially determine whether the equipment can be safely repaired.

Replacement may be considered when:

- The equipment cannot be safely repaired.
- Repeated faults make continued repair impractical.
- The equipment is no longer suitable for normal operation.
- Maintenance recommends replacement.

## Authorisation

Employees must **not independently order replacement equipment**.

Replacement equipment may only be ordered through the appropriate management or authorised procurement process.

A broken coffee machine does not automatically mean that a replacement should be purchased. The maintenance and authorisation process must be followed.
